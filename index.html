Graduation Project / Samsung SRI-Delhi

Use of Generative AI for enhancing Customer Satisfaction

A research-led UX project exploring how GenAI can reduce agent effort, connect fragmented service channels, and improve customer satisfaction across Samsung support workflows.

Primary research Call centre study Journey mapping Enterprise UX
  • Studied 15+ users, observed 25-30 support calls, and mapped agent and customer pain points across call, WhatsApp, email, chatbot, website, and service-centre channels.
  • Identified blockers including language handoffs, excessive manual typing, fragmented apps, repeated channel starts, duplicate profiles, and slow after-call work.
  • Proposed COSS, a one-stop agent workspace using speech-to-text, auto-summary, auto-fill, consolidated history, solution prompts, and progressive disclosure.
  • Projected a shift from 4-9 active apps to 1-2, reduced call handling time from 8-10 minutes to under 4 minutes, and improved agent efficiency by roughly 33%.
